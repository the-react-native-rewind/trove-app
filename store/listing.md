## Promotional text

Your groups each have a list. None of them show you only what is yours. Trove keeps the shared list, and gathers everything assigned to you in one place.

## Description

The work of a household, a garden crew, a choir, or a side project never lives in one place. It is split across group chats and shared boards, and the part that is actually yours is mixed in with everyone else's.

Trove is a shared list for each group you belong to. Everyone in the group sees the whole list. Mine is the other view: every task assigned to you, from every group, on one screen. Join a group and that view is already there.

A private Personal space comes with your account. Hand a task to someone else when it is no longer yours. When you are done with Trove, you can delete your account from inside the app.

## Age rating and privacy

No unrestricted web, no ads, no tracking. Account creation collects an email and a display name. People can add photos or videos to a task, and can dictate a task with on-device speech. Declare those as data linked to the user for app functionality, not used for tracking. Do not declare audio as collected data: speech recognition stays on the device. A privacy nutrition form is still something you complete in App Store Connect. The draft answers live next to this file in `privacy-policy.md`.
