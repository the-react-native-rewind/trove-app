## Description

Lying awake with 47 tabs open in your head? Close them in 10 minutes and start finishing what you start.

Trove is a to-do list for ADHD founders with too many projects. Built by one.

IT'S 2AM. NONE OF THE TABS WILL CLOSE.
The proposal you promised Tuesday. The side project you started Sunday. The tap that's still dripping. The call to your mum you meant to make last week. So you lie there, running the list. Again. Then morning comes, and you start something new instead.

It isn't a willpower problem. You've got drive to spare. You've just got nowhere to put it.

SAY IT OUT LOUD
Tap the mic and talk. Trove writes the task for you, and works out a title, notes and a due date if you said one. It's out of your head and on the list.

ONE LIST, TOP TO BOTTOM
Today pulls in what's yours from every project onto one list. The thing that matters goes on line one. Something urgent? Drag it up. Tick. Next.

YESTERDAY STAYS IN YESTERDAY
Every line has a day. Didn't finish? It stays on its day, and tomorrow you glance back and decide: bring it, or let it go. No red wall of overdue tasks. Swipe to tomorrow and write the first line tonight. Close the phone. Sleep.

THE PROJECTS YOU FINISH ARE THE ONES WITH SOMEONE WAITING
Every project gets a circle: the business, the house, the community night, the family. Invite the people in it by email or link. No training, no per-person cost. Put a name on a line and it leaves your list. Someone's waiting, so it moves.

ROOM FOR THE REST OF YOUR LIFE
Your private Personal circle sits on the same list as the business, so the yoga and the call to your mum stop losing every time. Set routines for the days you choose.

PHOTOS ON TASKS
Snap the leaking tap or the cracked tile and it's on the task, so whoever picks it up knows what they're looking at.

AN AGENT CAN TAKE A TASK
With Trove Pro, connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Grok and hand it a line, like “summarise the roof quotes”. It stays in the circle. You first, then your people, then an agent where it earns its place.

WHY OTHER TOOLS FAIL YOU
Notion asks you to build a system, then maintain it. Planners are beautiful for 14 days. To-do apps punish one missed day with a red wall. Most ADHD planners are built for one, so nobody's waiting. Trove isn't a bigger system. It's a smaller one, with people in it.

WHY I BUILT IT
“I've used Notion for almost a decade. I've spent several hundred hours in it, and I built my Life OS there. I've used every planning system going, from the Productivity Planner to the bullet journal. With some, you end up maintaining the system more than actually getting things done. I've got light ADHD, so much going on, and so much I want to build. So I built Trove.” Luke Farrell, founder

THE 14-DAY PROMISE
Give Trove ten minutes tonight. Use it for 14 days. Still opening it on day 14? It's working. If not, delete it. You can delete your account from inside the app.

Declare those as data linked to the user for app functionality, not used for tracking. Do not declare audio as collected data: speech recognition stays on the device.
